Program description

Ethan Fleck, an independent personal trainer, sends text messages from (808) 207-3183 to clients and contacts who opted in. Messages cover training session scheduling, confirmations and reminders, replies and coordination for requests the recipient made, and, for those who opted in to them, occasional updates about Ethan's training and coaching programs.

Opting in

You opt in by verbally agreeing when Ethan asks in person or by phone, or by texting START to (808) 207-3183.

Message frequency

Opting out

You can cancel at any time. Text STOP to (808) 207-3183. After you send STOP, we will send one message confirming you have been unsubscribed and you will receive no further messages. To join again, text START.
